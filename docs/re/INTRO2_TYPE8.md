# Native Type8 workers

This owns shared native Type8 birth, live task graphs, primary/infected hits and
class14 death continuation in `v2k-game::intro2_type8`, including its explicit
Intro2 replay constructor. The source contracts
shared with peasants remain in [ACTOR_RUNTIME.md](ACTOR_RUNTIME.md),
[ENTITY_DAMAGE_AND_DEATH.md](ENTITY_DAMAGE_AND_DEATH.md) and
[FACTORY_SYSTEM.md](FACTORY_SYSTEM.md). Main Base conversion and factory-ejected
workers retain their separate allocation histories through the completed-birth
seam. Intro2 capture evidence does not supply those workers' seeds or heap bytes.

## Authored record and allocation evidence

The normal-tier Section12 Type8 row has model slots `[559;4]`, capability
`0x1404`, health1500, mass10, effective constructor flags `0x2F`, alternate
class14, and the ordered root choices Job Nearby `(13,100,54)` followed by
Always `(1,1,6)`. Axis words are `0xF00/0x84`. Its common mover is exactly
Sub-D -> Sub-I -> Sub-A -> Sub-B. Sub-A is `(1500,-3000,250)`; the Sub-I
record has binding1, four frames per direction and all three sound words zero.
Type8's accepted-hit, infected-model, generic-hit, death and attached-loop
sound words are null. Its surface selector/lifetime is `[1,0]/5000ms`.

## Shared native construction and custody

The same normal-tier row occurs in the seven later authored workers: four in
level25 and three in39. `publish_authored_type8` receives the actual spawn,
terrain, linked predecessor list, manager allocation lease and successful
process-owned Sub-D construction. It preserves authored angle/parameter and
terrain model-domain bits, grounds Y through D4A0, performs the complete
Job Nearby/Always selection, then publishes the body basis and bit4. There is
no level, spawn-index or captured-seed admission list on this native route.

Dynamic factory and Main Base construction now admits the actual Type8/79/90/91/116
selected by world styles1–5. One shared birth transaction retains the selected
type's model, health, axis, choice order, D descriptor and I cues; it charges
the body stamp and successful D allocation, then the A20450, selector and
successful class6/54 suffix words in source order. `retain_native_type8_runtime`
accepts that completed class6/54 graph
and its matching Sub-D allocation, without repeating component construction,
selection or RNG. Its receipt permits an absent authored index, retains the
grounded anchor and exact birth task/context, and binds the manager allocation
generation. Shared scheduler adoption checks that allocation before consuming
the birth graph once. Legacy worker adoption excludes every native receipt,
including a rejected or already-consumed receipt; it cannot bypass custody.
Hits, standard death and parked callbacks retain the same allocation checks.
Factory owner-link/Type93 attachment and Main Base relation/operation0x33 retain
their distinct ordered tails. [Type7](TYPE7_RUNTIME.md) shares only this common
body phase; its own four-choice owner publishes the selected full graph and
query-free D receipt before the same caller-owned relation tail.

Native Sub-D uses the process owner's real successful-allocation seed and the
explicit `NativeFirstQueryReset` policy. A cleared cache anchors on its first
actual query; this does not claim that arbitrary retail allocator origins or
later cache-window placement match the Intro2 recording. Captured and known-
residue policies remain separate. Native B2 is deliberately initialized once
to zero because 104B0 has no writer for that transient heap word. This is a
deterministic allocation policy, independent of the observed Intro2 zero and
of scheduler wait/continue decisions. No captured first-scheduler overwrite
is applied; later callback mass uses current B2 and the original clear sites.

## Carrying and materialiser release

The actual factory ejection and player beam invoke the same generic relation
callbacks as ordinary Type8 workers. `16700` sets state1000 and parent `+80`,
then type-vtable `+44=DBF0` reaches class6/54 style `+08=CD50`. `CD70` accepts
capability1404 through maskC00, attaches the existing silent Sub-I through
20760, clears8000 and invokes C6B0/ADB0. The latter retires slots2/1 and replaces
Primary with the real None task. Carrying styles `4C7A08/4C87D0` have effective
flags `(2F|80)&!2=AD`; this is a graph replacement, not suspended Wander.

The attached worker still receives12DA0: scheduler waits/carry, relation age,
live parent/Sub-J membership, callback mass and the None03250/Sub-I callback
precede F70/E100/E370 and the master-motion gate. None advances Sub-I in both
detail modes and never calls Sub-D. AD skips DF70 ground snap; factory08F00
also clears master-motion40000. Missing/remote parents remain explicit
relation boundaries; attachment does not justify ignoring a blocked callback.

Playing retains the frame-entry animation claims and merges current claims
after cargo transfers and worker adoption, before the task pass. Thus a newly
adopted worker's first detailed or coarse callback also excludes the later
compatibility Sub-I pass.

Player drop has two release callbacks: release from the player before attaching
to the newly constructed Type93, then release from that proxy when settled.
`409030` writes both current position+96 and live Wander anchor+90, enables
master motion, and enters16750/DC50/CE90. CE90 sets8000, releases Sub-I and
selects a fresh class54/6 root, consuming its selector and constructor words
once. The first release retains the old anchor; the second uses the landing
anchor. Class6 reads live+90, not receipt birth evidence. Type93 and Type61
have no Sub-D descriptor, so their constructors do not advance its allocation
counter. Neither relation transition reconstructs the worker's Sub-D cache.

A private exact relation-graph publication transfers the completed task/context
to an already adopted scheduler owner, or updates a not-yet-adopted tail birth.
The manager lease remains unchanged; arbitrary replacement and parked owners
cannot use that receipt to bypass custody. Carrying clears8000, so checked
damage returns zero at `415060/415063` before packet filtering and leaves the
worker alive. The independent standard-death entry `10C10` has no8000 gate
(`410C4F..410C72`); it installs class14 and retains attachment. Class14 has null
hit/release callbacks, but CD50 selects variant1 (`4C7108`) on attachment.
CD70 first plays the child's Sub-I cue using the parent's capability and
position, links Sub-I, clears8000 and publishes the terminal context. C470 then
runs A860 (physical task slots0→1→2 with retirement) and410B70. The scheduler
or active cursor drops that owner immediately; the body and parent's Sub-J row
remain for ordinary cleanup. This consumes no selector RNG or second death cue.
A release without reattachment retains Class14 until its ordinary terminal.

If the worker dies before a nonterminal Type93 visit, `18640` retires its
Sub-J row before copying the proxy pose. The child's next continuing `12DA0`
finds its still-live parent without that row and calls `16750` through class14's
null release hook. The later empty `18500` cannot invoke `409030`, so this path
does not rewrite the child's position or live `+90` anchor to the landing point.
This order is established by the static consumers; the factory regression
checks row retirement before child-side release separately from living cargo's
normal materialiser release.

## Live tasks and world suffix

The owner runs the shared `12DA0` scheduler once. Its continuing delta is
separate from the process-global Sub-D delta; callback mass includes current
B2. Detailed mode calls `01430` with mode0, including Sub-I; coarse mode1
skips Sub-I while preserving D/A/B. Class6 uses live entity+90 and
class54 resolves its actual live target before `23030/01430`. The direct F70
basis follows the task's final Euler words, then E100, DF70 at old X/Z, E370
and master motion; effective2F does not enter E640.
The outer adapter authenticates the current level's environment and uses
shared44EC60 wind/drag with the actual callback mass, continuing delta and
frame-owned wind vector. F70 precedes the angular wind impulse: Euler words
change without rebuilding that retained basis. Sea-side and terrain shelter
gates remain active, and this actor callback never advances the gust phase.

The scheduler's `actor_animation_claims` includes these workers' exact native
allocation leases alongside its Type9 owners. Intro2's earlier neutral pass
and Playing's later neutral pass exclude those claims: detailed workers
receive only the task's Sub-I call, while coarse workers receive none. The
frame-entry claims survive waiting, blocked and dropped visits; Playing also
retains claims introduced by cargo publication. This does not admit any other
actor family into the specialized animation policy. The later-world
`intro2_type8::animation_tests` regressions exercise both host orders and a
blocked mover/dropped graph without replaying the compatibility callback.

Task elapsed and callback retarget/RNG commit before the atomic ABDI mover.
The exact descriptor and known classifier receipt close the kernel's later
Sub-D inputs. A blocked visit retains those task prefixes and parks; later
visits cannot repeat elapsed, retarget or RNG. E370 uses the common surface
algorithm with an explicit native-allocation/class14 policy. Existing common12
callers keep their original allocation and death policies. The order remains
timer -> release -> synchronous death -> bubble -> sound, and a death
publication survives a later blocked surface suffix.

## Hits and class14 death

The worker's native hit wrapper owns `10EB0/11250`, including the primary
timestamp or infected-model prefix. Live class6 style `004C79C0` and class54
style `004C8788` dispatch C690 in both primary `+28` and infected `+20` slots.
That selection precedes `11030` and the checked `15040/14E90` body, even for a
filtered-zero infection. It is not a fixed-actor survivor-policy exception.
Carrying styles `004C7A08/004C87D0` and class14 styles `004C70C0/004C7108`
have no hit callbacks, so later hits cannot
restart its timer. Canonical capability1404 has no primary capability8 suffix.

The observed class38 dragon packet has channels `[2,3]`, amounts `[500,6000]`:
Type8 filters it to `300+12000=12300`. A radial caller enters the native death
publisher after its own impulse/filter/health prefix; it never calls damage
again. `10C10` retains remote-owned and already-dying precedence, then writes
health0/dying and enters the null current-style death hook and alternate14.
There is no Type8 death sound or capability800 C6 notification. Health0/dying
remain committed if a later style or component boundary fails. A parked owner
retains the partial graph under its allocation lease, while a same-id worker
from a different level load cannot inherit that custody.

`C3A0` clears Secondary then Tertiary, resets the existing worker Sub-I to
special mode, clears state8000, prepares a 1000ms shared-retarget task, consumes
one shared Sub-A initializer word, writes target speed1 and replaces Primary.
The worker's live Sub-D, basis and other component state survive. `01120`
handles the callback tag before the strict `elapsed>1000ms` timeout and checks
owner-transition suppression after unwind. Class14 completion selects variant1
and `C470`, which clears slots `1 -> 2 -> 0`, marks deferred destruction and
queues its count; the current world suffix still completes before removal.
